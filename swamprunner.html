Swamp Runner 🦎

A Nature Adventure Platformer

🐱 🦢 🐚 🍠

🎯 Your Mission:

  • 🍠Collect all 5 yams scattered across the swamp
  • 🐱Pet all 3 friendly cats (they give bonus points!)
  • Defeat the BOSS GOOSE by jumping on its head 3 times
  • ️Avoid regular swans - they'll make you lose points!

🎮 How to Play:

Mobile: Use the ← ↑ → buttons below the game

Desktop: Use Arrow Keys or WASD + Space to jump

Victory Reward: After defeating the boss, watch two sandhill cranes sing a unity song, then float away in a dreamboat to the magical cloud lake! ☁️🚣
